**Creditaitons and Usage!**

This application and tool is specifically for university/lower educative pupils who are in the mindset to using arduino, ESP32, STM32, microcontrollers.
Its very narrow in design as made from me straight scratched research and implementation.

I wish to create this tool as an exetentsion to my learning outside of University and in addition to more learning and interfacing with other tools i plan to design. (TBC)

Completely open sourced to anyone who wishes to contribute their findings and ideas - however judged implementations are to be discouraged by axledude.

Some extra details about the setup and usage on this project...
IDE: CLion + PlatformIO (plugin)
OrigiBoard: STM32 Nucleo G071RB
CLI: ArduinoCLI, Espressif
